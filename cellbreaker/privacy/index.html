Privacy policy — Cell Breaker

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy explains what data the Cell Breaker app, by Brunelli Games (Plínio Brunelli), uses and why.

1. Data stored on your device

The game stores on your device: settings, high scores (with the name you type), the game in progress, level progress and stored tools. This data is not sent to us or to third parties and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows Google AdMob ads (banner, ads between games and the optional ad to earn tools). To show and measure ads, Google may collect data such as the device advertising ID, IP address, device and system information and ad interactions.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy.
  • You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in Android settings.
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. "Remove ads" purchase

The purchase is made through Google Play. Payment and its data stay with Google; the app only receives confirmation that the purchase exists, to remove the ads.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: loading ads and checking the purchase. The game works offline.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

6. Your rights

Since we receive no personal data from you, there is no account on our side to access or delete. Game data stays on your device and you can delete it at any time. For data processed by Google, use Google's tools or contact us.
